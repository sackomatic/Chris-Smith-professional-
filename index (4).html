About Me

A local expert

I'm Christopher Smith, a web professional based right here in Princeton, NC. I started CS Web Solutions because I saw too many great local businesses either without a website, or stuck with something outdated that didn't reflect what they actually do.


I work one-on-one with every client — no call centers, no outsourcing. When you reach out, you get me directly. Whether you're a restaurant, a contractor, a salon, or any other local business, I'll build and maintain something you're proud to share with customers.

  • Personal & Local You'll always deal directly with me — a real person from your community who cares about your success.

What I Offer

Everything your business
needs to shine online.

Simple, affordable web services built for small and local businesses in the Princeton area. No tech jargon — just results you can see.

01

New Website Design & Build

Don't have a website yet — or embarrassed by your current one? I'll design and build you a clean, professional site from scratch that looks great on phones, tablets, and computers. Includes your branding, contact info, photos, and anything else you need to make a great first impression.

02

Website Hosting

Once your site is live, it needs a reliable home on the internet. I offer affordable hosting plans that keep your site fast, secure, and online 24/7. No complicated dashboards to manage — I handle it all so you can focus on running your business.

03

Website Updates & Edits

Businesses change — and your website should keep up. Need to update your hours, add new services, swap out photos, post an announcement, or adjust your pricing? Just send me a message and I'll get it updated quickly, usually same or next business day.

04

Tech Support

Something not working right on your site? Getting error messages, having trouble with your business email, or just confused about something tech-related? I'm here to help. Reach out by phone, text, or email and I'll walk you through it — plain and simple, no tech jargon.
